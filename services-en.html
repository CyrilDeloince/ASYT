Services

Five practices, one through line: the sovereignty of your data.

From strategic scoping to technical deployment, all the way to training your teams — we cover the entire lifecycle of an AI project, while staying inside your trust perimeter.

01 / Advisory

AI Strategy & Scoping

Before any deployment, understand. We work with your teams to identify use cases where AI delivers real value — and those where it introduces disproportionate risk.

  • Audit of candidate AI use cases within your organization
  • Mapping of sensitive data and their flows
  • Risk assessment: confidentiality, compliance, intellectual property
  • Technology and architecture recommendations
  • Prioritized deployment roadmap
02 / Build

Private & On-Premise AI

The heart of our work: deploying AI systems entirely hosted within your own infrastructure. Your data never transits through public APIs.

  • Selection and fine-tuning of open-source models (Mistral, Llama, Qwen…)
  • Deployment on your infrastructure (internal servers, air-gapped environments, local workstations)
  • Integration with your existing business tools (CRM, ERP, document management, email)
  • Setup of secure RAG on your document repositories
  • Full pipeline for training, evaluation and updates
03 / Audit

AI Security & Compliance Audit

Your teams may already be using AI tools — possibly without a formal framework. We assess your organization's real exposure and give you the tools to control it.

  • Mapping of current AI usage (official and shadow IT)
  • Tests for prompt injection, data leakage, jailbreaks of internal tools
  • Compliance analysis: GDPR, EU AI Act, sector-specific (HDS, ISO 27001…)
  • Usage policy, internal charter, and remediation plan
  • Executive debrief and prioritized action plan
04 / Transmission

Training & Cultural Onboarding

AI deployed without an accompanying culture becomes a mis-used tool. We train your teams — executives, business users, IT — to truly extract value from the solution we deploy.

  • Executive sessions for leadership teams (strategic understanding)
  • Operational, business-specific training tailored to your solution
  • Hands-on prompt-engineering workshops in a secured environment
  • Bespoke internal documentation and pedagogical materials
  • Internal AI champions program to make your teams self-sufficient
05 / Long term

Governance & Support

AI evolves fast. We stay at your side to evolve your setup, audit it periodically, and keep you informed of the advances that actually matter — without the media noise.

  • Setup of an AI governance committee
  • Targeted technology and regulatory watch
  • Maintenance and updates of deployed models
  • Recurring security and performance audits
  • Dedicated support and expert hotline
Methodology

How we work.

A structured five-step approach, designed to limit risk at every stage and to keep you in control of the decisions.

STEP · 01

Listen

We start by understanding your business, your regulatory constraints and the concrete challenges your teams face. No solution before a proper diagnosis.

STEP · 02

Scope

Precise definition of the perimeter, selected use cases, target architecture and success indicators. A shared document that serves as our reference.

STEP · 03

Prototype

Building a first usable deliverable within a few weeks, on a representative subset. The goal: validate in the field before industrializing.

STEP · 04

Deploy

Production rollout on your infrastructure, integration with existing tools, security testing, user acceptance, training of early adopters.

STEP · 05

Hand over

Complete documentation, in-depth team training, governance plan. You become autonomous — we remain available if needed.
