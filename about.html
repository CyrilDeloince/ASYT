About

Building AI where trust is the first requirement.

We are an engineering and consulting firm focused on one mission: enabling organizations that handle sensitive data to use artificial intelligence without sacrificing their sovereignty.

Why we exist

A simple conviction.

Consumer AI tools have changed the world. But they were not built for lawyers handling confidential files, for doctors handling patient records, for industrial groups holding strategic patents, nor for entrepreneurs who simply cannot afford a leak.

We created ASYT Associates for these organizations. The ones for which "uploading a file to a US chatbot" is not an option. The ones who want the benefits of AI without accepting its current risks around confidentiality, intellectual property and compliance.

What guides us

Four non-negotiable commitments.

COMMITMENT 01

Data sovereignty.

Your data never leaves your perimeter. Every deployment runs on your own infrastructure, in an environment you control.

COMMITMENT 02

Intellectual honesty.

If AI is not the right answer to your problem, we will tell you. We prefer a satisfied client over a sold project.

COMMITMENT 03

Technical mastery.

We only work with models we deeply understand. No black boxes, no empty promises.

COMMITMENT 04

Continuous teaching.

Our clients don't become dependent. We train your teams to understand, govern and evolve their AI tools.
